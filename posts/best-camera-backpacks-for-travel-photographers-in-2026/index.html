What to Look For

1. Fit: The backpack should fit your torso length, not just your height. Most brands offer size guides.

2. Access: Side access is essential for travel. You should be able to grab your camera without taking the bag off.

3. Protection: Look for padded dividers, weather-resistant materials, and a rain cover.

4. Comfort: Padded hip belt, sternum strap, and breathable back panel are non-negotiable for all-day carrying.

5. Security: Lockable zippers and hidden pockets for passports and cash.
