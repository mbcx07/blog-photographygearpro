Best Camera Backpacks for Travel Photographers in 2026

2026-07-20 · Cameras · Tags: camera bag, backpack, travel photography, gear storage
## Best Camera Backpacks for Travel A good camera backpack is the most important accessory you'll buy. It protects your gear, keeps you comfortable, and makes or breaks your travel photography experience. We tested 12 top backpacks to find the best. ### Peak Design Travel Backpack 45L — $300 **The best overall travel camera backpack.** The 45L expands from 35L to 45L, adapting to your needs. The camera cube (sold separately, $50) is removable, converting the bag into a regular travel backpack. The side access is fast and secure. **Pros:** - Expands/compresses for different trip lengths - Excellent build quality and weather resistance - Comfortable harness system for heavy loads - Clever organization with magnetic closures - Lifetime warranty **Cons:** - Camera cube is extra - Can look bulky when fully packed - Hip belt is minimal for heavy loads **Best for:** Serious travel photographers who need one bag for everything ### Wandrd PRVKE 31L — $250 **The best for adventure photography.** The PRVKE's roll-top expands from 21L to 31L. The side camera access is the fastest we've tested, and the removable waist belt adds stability for hiking. **Pros:** - Roll-top expansion is brilliant - Excellent side camera access - Built-in rain fly - Laptop sleeve fits 15" laptop - Stylish, modern design **Cons:** - Camera cube space is limited with the roll-top compressed - Waist belt is an add-on - No luggage pass-through **Best for:** Hikers, adventure photographers, urban explorers ### Lowepro ProTactic 450 AW II — $230 **The best for quick access.** The ProTactic's four access points (back, top, both sides) mean you never have to take the bag off to access gear. The modular organization system is the most customizable we've seen. **Pros:** - Four access points for lightning-fast gear changes - Modular dividers for custom organization - Built-in All Weather cover - Tripod attachment system - Excellent padding and protection **Cons:** - Heavy (4.5 lbs empty) - Not great as a general travel bag - Can look tactical/bulky **Best for:** Working photographers who need fast gear access ### Shimoda Explore V2 35L — $280 **The best for long treks.** Shimoda backpacks are designed by photographers who hike. The harness system is the most comfortable for heavy loads, and the removable camera unit lets you use it as a regular backpack. **Pros:** - Best-in-class harness for heavy loads - Removable ICU (Internal Camera Unit) - Excellent weather resistance - Multiple sizes and ICU options - Sustainable materials **Cons:** - Expensive with ICU - Complex organization system - Heavy (3.8 lbs empty) **Best for:** Photographers who hike long distances with heavy gear ### What to Look For **1. Fit:** The backpack should fit your torso length, not just your height. Most brands offer size guides. **2. Access:** Side access is essential for travel. You should be able to grab your camera without taking the bag off. **3. Protection:** Look for padded dividers, weather-resistant materials, and a rain cover. **4. Comfort:** Padded hip belt, sternum strap, and breathable back panel are non-negotiable for all-day carrying. **5. Security:** Lockable zippers and hidden pockets for passports and cash. ### Our Top Pick The **Peak Design Travel Backpack 45L** is the best all-around choice for most travel photographers. It's expensive, but the versatility, build quality, and lifetime warranty make it a worthwhile investment. If you hike with your gear, the **Shimoda Explore V2** is worth the extra cost for the superior harness system.
